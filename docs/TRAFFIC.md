# Signals log (December gate)

The browser page has no analytics by design, so "files validated in the browser" cannot be
counted and is not a gate signal. What remains is measurable and is noted here **every week**
(GitHub keeps Traffic for 14 days only; the log is the history).

Sources: `https://github.com/aeoi-com/aeoi/graphs/traffic` (Insights → Traffic: unique visitors,
unique cloners, referring sites), stars and issues by people not involved in the project,
`https://pypistats.org/packages/aeoi` (downloads *without mirrors*, only after the 0.0.1
release), requests that reach us through associations.

Gate (mid-December 2026), OR: ≥ 100 unique visitors in a 14-day window from at least three
referring sites, or ≥ 5 stars / ≥ 2 issues from strangers, or ≥ 200 pypistats downloads without
mirrors in a month, or ≥ 1 request through an association. Otherwise: pause after the pilot's
3.0 test (week of 16.01.2027) and reassess.
